ABOUT OUR AGENCY

OUR HISTORY

Founded in 2020, Football Agency Sierra Leone was established to create a professional pathway for the incredible untapped talent within the country. We are the first and only agency of our kind, fully licensed and dedicated to player management and development.

OUR VISION

Our vision is to see Sierra Leonean footballers competing at the highest levels of professional football globally. We aim to be the most trusted, transparent, and effective agency in West Africa, known for our integrity and player-first philosophy.

OUR MISSION

To identify, nurture, and manage the careers of promising footballers. We provide comprehensive support, including contract negotiation, financial planning, and personal development, ensuring our players can focus on achieving their full potential on and off the pitch.
